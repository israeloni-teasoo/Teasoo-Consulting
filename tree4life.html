Reforestation & Nature-Based Solutions

Tree4Life

Regreening Edo State — one million indigenous trees, restored forests and thriving communities.

Tree4Life is a landmark reforestation programme regreening degraded forest reserve in Edo State, Nigeria.

Teasoo Consulting designed and delivers the programme end to end — turning a corporate sustainability commitment into a standing, thriving forest.

From strategy to a standing forest, Teasoo is your partner.

Why it matters

A degraded forest, restored

Decades of pressure had thinned once-dense reserve — eroding soils, displacing wildlife and releasing stored carbon, with host communities feeling the loss first. Tree4Life reverses that trajectory.

Restored forest rebuilds soil, cools the land, shelters wildlife and draws carbon from the atmosphere — a nature-based response to the climate crisis that creates green livelihoods where they are needed most.

Your partner, end to end

From strategy to a standing forest

Teasoo Consulting takes reforestation commitments from ambition to lasting impact — shaping the strategy, mobilising communities and government, delivering on the ground and assuring the outcome.

Organisations partner with Teasoo when a pledge needs to become a credible, measurable forest — one that stands the test of decades and stands up to global scrutiny.

A global standard

Aligned to the global movement to prevent, halt and reverse the degradation of ecosystems through 2030 and beyond.

The Bonn Challenge & AFR100
