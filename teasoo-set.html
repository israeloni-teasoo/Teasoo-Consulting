What it does

Everything the relationship needs, in one place

From the field team's first note to the boardroom summary.

One authoritative directory

Every stakeholder in one tiered record. No more scattered spreadsheets or conflicting versions of the truth.

Fast field capture

The field team logs an engagement in seconds. Each log refreshes the record and keeps relationships from going stale.

Risk and escalations

High risk or a raised flag opens an escalation automatically, so the right people act before a problem grows.

Leadership visibility

Function heads and leadership see sentiment, risk and commitments across the portfolio, live rather than in a monthly deck.

Security and privacy

Built for organisations that take data protection seriously

Isolation and access control are enforced in the database, not just the interface.

Your data stays yours

Each organisation's data is walled off in the database, enforced by row-level security and tested continuously. One organisation can never see another's data.

Role-based access

People see only what their role allows. Access is decided in the database, not just hidden in the interface.

GDPR and NDPA aligned
