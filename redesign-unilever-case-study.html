The challenge

Reactive, siloed, and hard to defend

Unilever's teams were managing stakeholders reactively — inconsistent messaging, unclear ownership, and engagements tracked in memory and spreadsheets. In a complex Nigerian operating environment, that put the company's regulatory, social and political licence to operate at risk.

What we did
  • 01Reframed stakeholder management as a strategic, business-critical function.
  • 02Introduced standard tools — the Iceberg and P.I.N. models and a central Engagement Tracker.
  • 03Ran functional mapping and named-owner assignment across five teams.
  • 04Pressure-tested the skills in a live crisis simulation.

How we worked

Standard tools, so the shift outlasts the workshop

Teasoo delivered strategy, facilitation and tools in a single engagement — anchored by leadership “tone from the top.” Four frameworks now sit at the centre of how Unilever's teams engage.

Stakeholder Iceberg

Manage the 90% below the surface — informal influence and hidden grievances.

P.I.N. Model

Move past positions to the underlying interests and needs.

Influence–Interest Matrix

Prioritise stakeholders and assign clear, named owners.

Engagement Tracker

One central system for accountability and institutional memory.
