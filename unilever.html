Strategic Stakeholder Management Workshop

How Teasoo equipped Unilever Nigeria's teams to move from reactive troubleshooting to proactive, one-voice stakeholder management — and protect the company's licence to operate.

The challenge

Stakeholders managed reactively, not strategically

Unilever's cross-functional teams were troubleshooting problems as they arose — with inconsistent messaging, unclear ownership, and engagements tracked in memory and spreadsheets. That put the company's regulatory, social and political licence to operate at risk.

What we did

A shared language, standard tools, and pressure-tested skills

01

Reframed the discipline

Positioned stakeholder management as a core driver of business performance — not a support activity — with leadership setting the "tone from the top."

02

Introduced standard frameworks

Equipped teams with the Stakeholder Iceberg and P.I.N. models, and a central Engagement Tracker to enforce accountability and institutional memory.

03

Mapped and assigned ownership

Five functional groups mapped their stakeholder universe, prioritised with the Influence–Interest matrix, and assigned named owners to end ambiguity.

04

Pressure-tested in a crisis simulation

Teams navigated a live, escalating crisis — designing engagement strategies and an escalation pathway under real-time pressure.

The impact

A measurable shift in capability and confidence

The post-workshop assessment showed a decisive move from reactive to strategic — with tools the teams could apply immediately.

96%

rated the workshop Good to Excellent across both cohorts.

92%

now view stakeholder management as a core strategic function, not a reactive task.

96%

reported a stronger grasp of "One Voice" internal alignment.

100%

agreed the session will improve Unilever's organisational practices.
