What we do

End-to-end reforestation, delivered as one service

Everything a nature-based programme needs, from the first strategy session to a measured, carbon-ready forest.

Strategy & project design

We shape the programme end to end: objectives, site selection, species strategy, delivery plan and the governance that holds it together over decades.

Nursery establishment

We set up and run nurseries that produce healthy, locally adapted, indigenous seedlings at scale, so planting is supplied from a reliable, quality-controlled source.

Seedling distribution

We coordinate the supply and distribution of seedlings to planting sites and communities, including food trees that put value directly into local hands.

Stakeholder management & community engagement

We bring governments, host communities, academia and partners into one aligned programme, with sustained engagement that protects the licence to operate.

Capacity building & community empowerment

We train and equip local people with durable skills, green jobs and livelihoods, so communities own and sustain the forest long after planting.

Carbon offset & credit development

We develop the carbon side to international methodology, with the measurement, monitoring, reporting and verification support that makes credits credible.

Why organisations choose Teasoo

A pledge becomes a forest that lasts

Many reforestation commitments stall between ambition and the ground. Teasoo closes that gap, turning pledges into trees that are planted, protected, measured and built to stand the test of decades.

We work to global restoration standards and local realities at once, so your programme stands up to scrutiny from regulators, investors and communities alike.

Grown, not just bought

Nurseries built for healthy, indigenous trees

We establish and run nurseries that raise healthy, locally adapted, indigenous seedlings at scale, so every planting season is supplied from a reliable, quality-controlled source.

From seed sourcing to distribution, we keep the trees that go into the ground vigorous, native and ready to thrive.

People at the centre

Communities that own the forest

We bring host communities into the heart of every programme, with sustained engagement, local green jobs, training and food trees that put lasting value into local hands.

Capacity building and empowerment mean the skills, income and ownership stay long after the last tree is planted.

A global standard

Reforestation held to the world's benchmark

Our programmes are designed to sit alongside the leading nature-restoration work worldwide, and aligned to the frameworks that define credible, global reforestation and carbon.

UN Decade on Ecosystem Restoration

Aligned to the global movement to prevent, halt and reverse the degradation of ecosystems through 2030 and beyond.

The Bonn Challenge & AFR100

Contributing to Africa's ambition to bring 100 million hectares of degraded land into restoration.

Verra-aligned carbon

Carbon developed to recognised international methodology, with measurement and verification built in from the start.

Tree4Life

A landmark programme regreening degraded forest reserve in Edo State: one million indigenous trees across 6,000 hectares, restoring habitat, locking away carbon and creating green livelihoods for host communities.

Green Legacy
