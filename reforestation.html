Why it matters

Why reforestation, and why now

Reforestation is more than planting trees. It rebuilds the living systems that land, climate and communities depend on, and turns a climate commitment into something that lasts.

Forests are life-support

A healthy forest rebuilds soil, cools and cleans the air, shelters wildlife and protects water, while giving communities food, income and shelter. When forest is lost, all of that goes with it.

Trees are carbon, captured

As they grow, trees draw carbon dioxide from the air and lock it away in wood and soil. Measured and verified to international methodology, that carbon becomes credits that fund the planting and the people who protect it.

Restoration that lasts

New forests take decades to mature and only count if they are never cleared again. That is why we plant diverse native species, protect what still stands, and govern every programme for the long term.

Edo State: the case for action

One of Nigeria's deforestation hotspots

Edo State holds some of Nigeria's richest rainforest, yet it is losing it faster than almost anywhere in the country. Decades of logging and farmland conversion have thinned its reserves, eroded soils and pushed wildlife out.

Tree4Life restores that degraded reserve with indigenous, mixed-species forest, rebuilding canopy, soil and water cycles, bringing back birds and wildlife, and reconnecting habitat on the land that was being lost fastest.

Carbon credits & climate goals

Turn a growing forest into carbon credits

For companies, reforestation is more than good for the planet. The carbon your forest captures can be verified and issued as carbon credits: a credible way to offset emissions and meet climate commitments.

Emissions to offset

Your organisation has a carbon footprint it needs to reduce and offset.

Forests capture it

As the trees grow, they draw CO₂ from the air and lock it into wood and soil.

Verified as credits

The carbon is measured and verified to international methodology. One credit equals one tonne of CO₂.

Offset & comply

Retire the credits to offset emissions and strengthen your sustainability and reporting.
